# Audio in the editing pipeline: spec

Status: agreed in conversation on 2026-10-15. Phase 1 (music track) is planned
in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-music-beat-sync.md`. Phases 2-4 get their
own plans once phase 1 has landed, because they reuse its plumbing.

## Today

The source audio rides along with the picture: trimmed with each clip,
concatenated at cuts, crossfaded under dissolves, faded in/out with the
timeline. The only audio analysis is a per-shot RMS loudness in the shot
catalog. No music, no sound design, no beat alignment, no dialogue awareness.

## Phase 1: prerecorded music track (this plan)

Requirements:

1. `python -m matchcut.pipeline run footage.mp4 --music track.mp3` plays the
   track under the cut, starting at `--music-start` seconds into the track.
2. The track is analysed once (cached in the workspace as `music.json`):
   tempo, beat times, downbeat times (every N beats, phase chosen by onset
   strength), and an RMS energy curve.
3. The narrative stage is told about the music (tempo, length, energy curve
   sampled every 5 s, where it rises and drops) so beats of the story can
   line up with sections of the track. The narrative schema does not change.
4. A new `beatsync` pass, running last (after `polish`, so dissolves and
   polish trims cannot move a cut off the grid afterwards), moves every cut
   onto the nearest beat (downbeat at beat changes when one
   is within reach). A cut moves by adjusting the outgoing clip's out-point,
   or its in-point when the out-point is locked by a match cut. Locked points
   never move. A cut never moves more than `snap_window` seconds. The pass is
   a no-op when no music is configured, so it can sit in the default list.
5. The render mixes music under the source audio: music gain in dB, optional
   sidechain ducking (source audio compresses the music), fade-out at the end
   of the cut, optional EBU R128 loudness normalisation. `music.only` drops the
   source audio. Footage without an audio stream gets music only.
6. The report gains a "Music" section: track, tempo, how many cuts were
   snapped and by how much on average.
7. Everything is testable offline: a synthetic click track generated by
   ffmpeg's `aevalsrc` stands in for real music.

Non-goals for phase 1: music sections detection beyond an energy curve,
tempo changes within the track, centring dissolves on beats (dissolves end
on the beat, as the render already works), EDL audio events.

Dependencies: `librosa>=0.10` (beat tracking, onset strength); decoding stays
on ffmpeg so no soundfile/audioread backends are needed.

## Phase 2: dialogue safety (future plan)

Whisper (faster-whisper) word timestamps per shot. Pacing and beatsync refuse
to place a cut inside a word or within 150 ms of one; transitions can start
audio before picture (J cut) or hold it after (L cut) by a configurable
lead/lag. Transcript lines go into the shot catalog so the narrative stage
can use what is said.

## Phase 3: sound effects (future plan)

A folder of SFX with tags (filename or a sidecar JSON). After polish, Claude
picks at most one cue per clip from the shot description, with a volume and
an offset. The render mixes cues as extra inputs in the same `amix`.

## Phase 4: generated music (future plan)

A `MusicSource` interface with two backends: `file` (phase 1) and
`generate`. The generator takes the narrative (tone, pace, target duration)
and writes a prompt for a local model (MusicGen / Stable Audio Open) or a
hosted API, saves the result as a file, and phase 1 takes over from there.
