# Edit tab screenshots

Captured headless with `python3 tools/capture_edit.py WORKSPACE docs/screenshots --junctions 3`
(a 140x46 terminal, half-block images). Both are the final cut of the 20 s Big
Buck Bunny clip from a mock-backend run (`-t 12 --no-render`): the frames and the
scores are real, the descriptions and narrative are mock, and the status bar says
so. The clip has three shots, so there are two junctions and the tool writes two
files.

- `edit-junction-1.svg`: junction 1 (shot 0 to shot 1), a beat boundary with a 0.75 s dissolve: outgoing frame, incoming frame, 50% overlay, scores, cut points, why and history.
- `edit-junction-2.svg`: junction 2 (shot 1 to shot 2), one `→` later, with the same layout.

# Review (M2b) images

Made headless with `tools/demo_review.py` (see [../M2B-ACCEPTANCE.md](../M2B-ACCEPTANCE.md) for the commands and what they do and do not show). The contact sheets are the frames a real mpv wrote with `--vo=image` while the review controller played A (top half) and then toggled to B (bottom half); the caption bars are drawn by the tool. Nothing was played on a screen.

- `m2b-review-bbb-j1.jpg`: Big Buck Bunny, junction 1 of a mock-backend run: A is the rough cut's hard cut (an mpv EDL over the source), B the final cut's 0.75 s dissolve (a rendered window).
- `m2b-edit-review.svg`: the Edit tab of the same workspace after `p`, with the review status line; the controller's mpv session is a stub.
- `m2b-review-synthetic-j3.jpg`, `m2b-review-synthetic-j4.jpg`: generated test footage (colour bars, test patterns, a fractal; `matchcut.pipeline.demo`): a hard cut against a 0.75 s dissolve, and a hard cut played exactly on both sides.
